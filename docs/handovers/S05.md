# S05 — Dark shell and virtual worksheet

Status: complete  
Date: 2026-11-17

## Delivered

- The production entry opens a blank workbook from `WorkbookSession`. It exposes black title, ribbon and formula bars, a worksheet strip, Quick Access Undo/Redo, a name box, a read-only formula bar, view toggles, zoom and selection statistics. Ribbon families without implemented commands show their availability.
- The canvas projects A1:Z1000 with 26 px rows and 96 px columns at 100%. Browser-native scrolling, fixed headers, hit testing and keyboard reveal share one coordinate map. Selection supports keyboard and drag extension, whole axes, and disjoint ranges. [Projection tests](../../tests/unit/grid-projection.test.ts)
- The semantic grid exposes the active cell even when scrolled offscreen. Tab moves within the worksheet, and Escape then Tab moves focus out. Forced colors use system palette colors for both chrome and canvas.
- Updated F03, F04, F18, F20 and F33 with scoped evidence. Verify CI runs the production browser suite and the separately built worksheet fixture.
